\section{Notation Table}
\label{app:notation}

Table~\ref{tab:notation_full} lists in full the notation used in the
mathematical model of Appendix~\ref{app:formal}.

\begin{table}[H]
  \centering
  \caption{Notation of the formal model (2-player mode).}
  \label{tab:notation_full}
  \begin{tabular}{ll}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Symbol} & \textbf{Meaning} \\
    \midrule
    $\mathcal B_{\mathsf V},\ \mathcal B_{\mathsf C},\ \mathcal B$
      & vertex grid (25), centre cells (16), whole board (41 cells) \\
    $\kappa(\cdot)$
      & type (vertex $\mathsf V$ / centre $\mathsf C$) of a cell or piece \\
    $\mathrm Y,\ \mathrm G,\ \bar c,\ \varnothing$
      & Yellow, Green, the opponent of $c$, empty cell \\
    $x_p,\ c_p$
      & current position and side of piece $p$ \\
    $P^{\mathrm Y}_0,\ P^{\mathrm G}_0$
      & the two sets of 14 starting cells for Yellow / Green \\
    $s=(b,\mathrm{turn})$
      & state: board configuration $b$ and side to move; $s_0$ is state 0 \\
    $N_c(s)$
      & number of pieces of side $c$; always $N_{\mathrm Y}{+}N_{\mathrm G}=28$ \\
    $D_{\mathsf V},\ D_{\mathsf C}$
      & movement directions of a vertex / centre piece (8 / 4 directions) \\
    $\Delta_{\mathsf V},\ \Delta_{\mathsf C}$
      & capturing directions of a vertex / centre piece (8 / 4 directions) \\
    $\mathrm{Jumps}(x,b)$
      & the valid landing cells of the piece at $x$ \\
    $\mathcal A_{\mathrm{move}}(s),\ \mathcal A(s)$
      & the moves; the full action set $(x,x',\delta)$ \\
    $F_\delta,\ \Delta(s,x,x')$
      & the run captured along direction $\delta$; the set of capturing directions \\
    $|a|$
      & action size $=$ number of pieces captured $=|F_\delta|$ \\
    $r_c(s,a)$
      & per-step reward: $+|a|$ for the mover, $-|a|$ for the other side \\
    $W$
      & terminal reward $\pm W$, with $W>0$ abstract \\
    $\mathrm{alive}(c,s)$
      & side $c$ still has a piece with a legal jump \\
    $T(s,a)$
      & transition function: jump $\to$ flip along $\delta$ $\to$ pass turn \\
    $\mathcal G$
      & $(\mathcal S,\mathcal A,T,r,R,s_0)$---the whole game \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

\section{Multiplayer Starting Configurations}
\label{app:multistart}

To specify the rules completely for 2--4 players (contribution~(i)), this
appendix lists the exact board and starting positions of the 3- and
4-player modes, matching the open-source implementation. Integer coordinates
are vertex cells, half-integer coordinates are centre cells. The 2-player
starting position was given in~\eqref{eq:starty}--\eqref{eq:startg}.

\paragraph{3-player mode.} A $7\times6$ vertex board ($x\in0\text{--}6$,
$y\in0\text{--}5$); four cells removed (no landing):
$(0,5),(0.5,4.5),(6,5),(5.5,4.5)$. Turn order: Purple $\to$ Yellow $\to$
Green.
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Yellow (13 pieces):} vertices $(0,0\text{--}4)$ and
        $(1,0\text{--}3)$; centres $(0.5,0.5)$, $(0.5,1.5)$, $(0.5,2.5)$, $(0.5,3.5)$.
  \item \textbf{Green (13 pieces):} vertices $(6,0\text{--}4)$ and
        $(5,0\text{--}3)$; centres $(5.5,0.5)$, $(5.5,1.5)$, $(5.5,2.5)$, $(5.5,3.5)$.
  \item \textbf{Purple (14 pieces):} vertices $(1\text{--}5,5)$ and
        $(1\text{--}5,4)$; centres $(1.5,4.5)$, $(2.5,4.5)$, $(3.5,4.5)$, $(4.5,4.5)$.
\end{itemize}
Note that this configuration is \emph{unbalanced} in piece count (13/13/14);
see Section~\ref{sec:limitations}.

\paragraph{4-player mode.} A $7\times7$ vertex board ($x,y\in0\text{--}6$);
eight cells removed at the four corners: $(0,0)$, $(0.5,0.5)$, $(6,0)$,
$(5.5,0.5)$, $(0,6)$, $(0.5,5.5)$, $(6,6)$, $(5.5,5.5)$. Each side has
\textbf{13 pieces}. Turn order: Blue $\to$ Green $\to$ Purple $\to$ Yellow.
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Blue (13):} vertices $(1\text{--}5,0)$ and $(1\text{--}4,1)$;
        centres $(1.5,0.5)$, $(2.5,0.5)$, $(3.5,0.5)$, $(4.5,0.5)$.
  \item \textbf{Yellow (13):} vertices $(0,1\text{--}5)$ and $(1,2\text{--}5)$;
        centres $(0.5,1.5)$, $(0.5,2.5)$, $(0.5,3.5)$, $(0.5,4.5)$.
  \item \textbf{Purple (13):} vertices $(1\text{--}5,6)$ and $(2\text{--}5,5)$;
        centres $(1.5,5.5)$, $(2.5,5.5)$, $(3.5,5.5)$, $(4.5,5.5)$.
  \item \textbf{Green (13):} vertices $(6,1\text{--}5)$ and $(5,1\text{--}4)$;
        centres $(5.5,1.5)$, $(5.5,2.5)$, $(5.5,3.5)$, $(5.5,4.5)$.
\end{itemize}
